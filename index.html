Credentials
{{ c }}

Steven H. Turner, JD — airline captain and aviation attorney.

Most aviation lawyers have read about the industry. Steven H. Turner has flown it — commanding Boeing 747s across the world's airspace while building a legal practice devoted to the people and businesses that make aviation run. That dual perspective, cockpit and courtroom, is what clients come to MPASS Law for.

Steven's path began in the United States Marine Corps, where he served eight years as an intelligence analyst — the foundation of the disciplined, detail-first approach he brings to every matter. As an ATP-rated captain, he has lived the operational realities his clients face — FAA regulation, safety management, crew decision-making, and the day-to-day pressures of running an aircraft or an aviation business. As an attorney, he translates that experience into transactions, structures, and counsel that actually hold up in the real world.

The result is legal work without the translation layer. Clients don't have to explain their world to their lawyer — their lawyer has been in the seat.
